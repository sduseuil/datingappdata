# Case Studies in the Analysis of Experimental Data -  Dating App Research Repository
***This repository contains all used materials and information surrounding the aformentioned study***
## Authors
This research was conducted in a collaborative effort between Lissa Vandoorne, Jef Van den Branden and Sarah Du Seuil in 2026. The original study was conducted by De La Mare and Lee in 2023.
## Purpose of the Research
A dataset containing rating data (rate: would you match or not 1-5) of over 800 participants of 200 profile pictures and 100 descriptions in a simulated dating app, originally created to study personality compatibility, was re-analysed to fit a linear mixed effects model concerning the relative contribution of pictures, descriptions and individual differences in rating schema to the rating of these profiles.
